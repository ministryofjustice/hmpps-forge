# GOV.UK style guide: A to Z

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide/a-to-z-of-gov-uk-style

---

## A

**A\*, A\*s** - Use the symbol \* not the word 'star'. No apostrophe in plural.

**A level** - No hyphen. Lower case level.

**Abbreviations, acronyms and initialisms** - Explain in full on first use, then use initials. Always use acronym Markdown. No full stops in abbreviations (BBC, not B.B.C.). Common abbreviations that need no explanation include BBC, CCTV, DIY, DVLA, EU, GP, HM, HTML, ID, MI5, MI6, MOT, MP, NHS, PAYE, SORN, TV, UK, URL, USA, VAT, VPN.

**The academies programme, academy, academy converters, academy order, academy trust** - All lower case.

**Access to Work** - Upper case when referring to the actual programme, otherwise lower case.

**Act, act of Parliament** - Lower case. Upper case only with full title (Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004).

**Active voice** - Use active rather than passive voice for clearer, more concise content.

**Addresses in the UK** - Start each part on new line. Write town and postcode separately. No commas at end of lines. Write country after postcode, not before. Only include country if user might write from different country.

**Addressing the user** - Use "you" where possible. Avoid gendered pronouns like "he" and "she".

**Adviser** - Not "advisor". "Advisory" is correct adjective form.

**Ages** - No hyphens unless avoiding confusion. Use "aged 4 to 16 years" not "4-16 years". Avoid "the over 50s" or "under-18s"; instead use "aged 50 years and over" and "aged 17 and under".

**Agile** - Upper case when referring to Agile Manifesto and principles. Lower case otherwise.

**Allow list** - Use as noun. Use allow as verb. Do not use "white list" or "whitelist".

**Al-Qa'ida** - Not "al-Qaeda" or "al-Qaida".

**American and UK English** - Use UK English spelling (organise not organize, modelling not modeling, fill in not fill out). American proper nouns take American spelling.

**Ampersand** - Use "and" rather than "&" unless it's a department's logo or company name as appears on Companies House register.

**Antisocial** - No hyphen.

**A-road** - Hyphenated.

**Armed forces** - Lower case.

**Arm's length body** - Apostrophe, no hyphen.

**Artificial intelligence** - Write first as "artificial intelligence (AI)" then "AI" throughout.

**Attendance Allowance** - Upper case.

## B

**Bacs (Bankers Automated Clearing System)** - Acronym comes first as more widely known than full name.

**Bank details** - Use bullet points and lead-in line, not table. Use spaces in sort codes (60 70 80 not 60-70-80). Avoid spaces in account numbers unless very long.

**Baseline** - One word, lower case.

**Billions** - Always use billion in money: £138 billion. Use billions in phrases: billions of people. Do not use £0.xx billion for amounts under £1 billion unless in sequence with amounts above. Do not abbreviate to "b".

**Blog post** - Two words. A "blog" is the site; "blog post" is the article.

**Board** - Lower case unless part of proper title (Judicial Executive Board). Upper case for specific boards, lower case for departmental or management boards.

**Bold** - Use only for interface elements in instructional text (Select **Start**). Use single quotes for interface elements in non-instructional contexts ('Done' button). Avoid over-use.

**Brackets** - Use (round brackets). Do not use for singular/plural options. Use [square brackets] for explanatory notes or placeholder text.

**Brexit** - Use to provide historical context, but prefer specific dates (31 December 2020 rather than "Brexit").

**British citizen, British national, British people** - Reference by activity where possible (British tourists). Use "British people" for general sense. Use "British nationals" only in legal contexts. Do not use "British citizen" unless referring to specific nationality type.

**Bullet points and steps** - Use bullet points to improve readability. Always use lead-in line. Use more than one bullet. Bullets should follow lead-in line grammatically. Use lower case at start. Use one sentence per bullet or use commas/dashes. No "or" or "and" after bullets. No semicolon at end. No full stop after last bullet. For steps: use numbered steps for processes. Steps end in full stop as complete sentences.

## C

**Cabinet** - Lower case.

**Capital Gains Tax** - Upper case.

**Capitalisation** - Use sentence case throughout. Exceptions for proper nouns: departments, Civil Service, job titles, titles (Mr, Mrs, Dr), buildings, place names, brand names, faculties, groups, organisations, Parliament, act/bill titles, named government schemes, select committees, table headers, book titles, World War 1/2.

Do not capitalise: government, minister (unless specific title), department/ministry (unless specific), white paper, green paper, command paper, budget, autumn/spring statements, sections/schedules in acts, director general, group/directorate (unless specific), policy themes, select committees (unless specific), military.

**Government departments** - Use conventions: Attorney General's Office (AGO), Cabinet Office (CO), Department for Business and Trade (DBT), Department for Culture, Media and Sport (DCMS), Department for Education (DfE), Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ), Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Defra), Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT), Department for Transport (DfT), Department for Work and Pensions (DWP), Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC), Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), HM Treasury (HMT), Home Office (HO), Ministry of Defence (MOD), Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG), Ministry of Justice (MOJ).

**Checkbox** - Not "check box".

**Civil Service** - Upper case. "Civil servants" lower case.

**Click** - Avoid. Use "select" instead, as not all users click. "Right-click" acceptable for specific interactions.

**CO2** - Capital letters with regular 2.

**Contractions** - Avoid negative contractions (can't, don't). Avoid complex/conditional contractions (should've, could've, would've).

**Co-operation** - Hyphenated.

**Corporation Tax** - Upper case.

**Council** - Lower case for local councils in general. Capitals for official names (Reading Borough Council).

**Council Tax** - Upper case.

**COVID-19** - Upper case. Do not use Covid-19, covid-19, or coronavirus.

## D

**Data** - Treat as singular: "The data is stored..."

**Data centre** - Not "datacentre". Two words.

**Data set** - Not "dataset". Two words.

**Data store** - Not "datastore". Two words.

**Dates** - Upper case months (January, February). No comma between month and year (4 June 2017). Truncate months in tables/titles (Jan, Feb). Use "to" in ranges, not hyphens (tax year 2011 to 2012). Do not use quarter; use months. Include date for "today" (14 June 2012). Use "on or before" for deadlines.

**Department** - Lower case except in full title (Department of Health and Social Care).

**Direct Debit** - Upper case.

**Disabled people** - Not "the disabled" or "people with disabilities".

## E

**Eg, etc and ie** - Avoid "eg" (read as "egg" by screen readers); use "for example", "such as", "like", or "including". Avoid "etc"; use alternatives. Avoid "ie"; rewrite sentences instead.

**Email** - One word. Write addresses in full, lower case, as active links.

**Endpoint** - Not "end point" (APIs context).

**Ethnic minorities** - Refer individually rather than as single group. Avoid BAME and BME terms.

## F

**FAQs (frequently asked questions)** - Do not use on GOV.UK.

**Fine** - Use instead of "financial penalty".

**Foundation trust** - Lower case except full names (Salisbury NHS Foundation Trust).

**Free school meals** - Lower case.

**Freedom of Information** - You make a Freedom of Information (FOI) request.

**Frontend** - Not "front-end" or "front end".

## G

**GCSE, GCSEs** - No full stops. No apostrophe in plural.

**Gender** - Use gender-neutral language (them, their, they). Use "women" and "men", not "males" and "females".

**Geography and regions** - Lower case for compass directions (north, south, east, west) except in names/regions. Upper case for: East End, West End, East Midlands, West Midlands, Middle East.

**Government** - Lower case unless full title (UK government, but His Majesty's Government...). Welsh Government upper case (full title).

**GOV.UK** - All upper case.

**Great Britain** - England, Scotland, Wales only (not Northern Ireland).

## H

**Headteacher** - One word.

**Homepage** - Lower case.

**HTTPS** - Upper case.

**Hyphenation** - Hyphenate: re- words with e (re-evaluate), co-ordinate, co-operate. Do not hyphenate: reuse, reinvent, reorder, reopen, email. Do not hyphenate unless confusing (little-used car vs little used car). Use "to" for time and date ranges, not hyphens.

## I

**Internet** - Lower case.

**Italics** - Do not use. Use 'single quotation marks' instead.

## J

**Job titles** - Upper case for specific titles (Minister for Housing). Lower case for generic (director, minister).

## K

**Key stage** - Lower case with numeral (key stage 4).

**The King** - Upper case K, lower case t.

## L

**Law** - Lower case even for "the law".

**Life cycle** - Not "lifecycle" or "life-cycle".

**Links** - Front-load with relevant terms. Make active and specific.

**Local authority** - Lower case. Do not use LA. Use "local council" instead where possible.

**Log in** - See sign in entry. Use "sign in" not "log in".

## M

**Measurements** - Use numerals and spell out at first mention. No space between numeral and abbreviation (3,500kg). Use Celsius (37°C).

**Metadata** - Not "meta data".

**Millions** - Always use million in money: £138 million. Use millions in phrases: millions of people. Do not abbreviate to "m".

**Money** - Use £ symbol (£75). No decimals unless pence included (£75.50 not £75.00). Write pence in full (4 pence per minute).

## N

**N/A** - Do not use in table cells. Use "no data" or "not applicable".

**National curriculum** - Lower case.

**National Insurance** - Upper case.

**.NET** - For programming language, not ".net" or ".Net".

**Numbers** - Use "one" except in steps, lists, or where numeral makes sense. Use numerals for 2 to 9 except common expressions ("one or two of them"). Write out numbers starting sentences except in titles/subheadings. Insert comma in numerals over 999 (9,000). Spell out fractions (one-half). Use % for percentages (50%). Use 0 before decimal (0.5 not .5). Use "500 to 900" not "500-900" except tables.

**Ordinal numbers** - Spell out first to ninth. Use 10th, 11th onwards. Use numerals throughout in tables.

## O

**Online** - One word.

**Open source software** - Not capitalised or abbreviated.

**Or** - Do not use slash instead of "or".

**Organisations** - Use singular verb for organisation names. Use "they" replacing organisation names.

## P

**Parliament** - Upper case.

**Per cent** - Not "percent". "Percentage" one word. Always use % with number.

**Plain English** - Lower case except in titles. Mandatory for GOV.UK.

**Please** - Usually unnecessary.

**Pull request** - Lower case (same as GitHub).

## Q

**Quotes and speech marks** - Single quotes for unusual terms (first mention only), quotations in titles, individual words/buttons, publication titles. Double quotes only for direct quotations. Use block quote Markdown for lengthy quotes.

## R

**RESTful** - In APIs context, not "restful" or "Restful".

## S

**Seasons** - Lower case (spring, summer, autumn, winter).

**Semicolons** - Do not use; often mis-read. Break into separate sentences instead.

**Sentence length** - Check sentences over 25 words for possible splitting.

**Sign in or log in** - Use "sign in" not "log in" for verb. Do not use "login" as noun unless part of name. Say what user enters (username, password).

**Spaces** - One space after full stop, not two.

**Subdomain** - Not "sub domain" or "sub-domain".

## T

**T Level** - No hyphen. Upper case Level.

**Telephone numbers** - Separate area/code from rest: 0131 496 0454, 020 7946 0457. Group memorable numbers: 0800 80 70 60.

**Temperature** - Use Celsius (37°C).

**Times** - Use "to" in ranges (10am to 11am, not 10-11am). 5:30pm (not 1730hrs). midnight (not 00:00). midday (not 12 noon). Include "UK time" for international users.

**Titles** - Pages: 65 characters maximum. Unique, clear, descriptive. Front-loaded, search-optimised. No full stop. Not questions.

**Trade marks** - Avoid branded names (tablet not iPad). Two words: "trade mark". One word: "trademarked".

## U

**UK government** - Never "HM government".

**USA** - Upper case. Not "US".

**Username** - Not "user name".

## V

**VPN** - Upper case.

## W

**Webchat** - One word.

**Webpage** - One word.

**White paper** - Lower case.

**Wifi** - Lower case, no hyphen.

**Words to avoid** - Use plain English. Avoid: agenda, advance, collaborate, combat, commit/pledge, counter, deliver, deploy, dialogue, disincentivise, empower, facilitate, focus, foster, impact, incentivise, initiate, key, land, leverage, liaise, overarching, progress, promote, robust, slim down, streamline, strengthening, tackle, transform, utilise.

Avoid metaphors: drive, drive out, going forward, hub, portal, one stop shop, in order to, ring fencing.

## X

**X account** - Upper case X. Formerly Twitter. Use "post on X" not "tweet".

## Z

**Zero-hours contract** - Not "zero-hour contract" or "zero hours contract".
